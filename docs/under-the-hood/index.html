Under the Hood

Five short guides that explain how StreamOtter works without reading every line of code. StreamOtter is one Node gateway that turns Kafka records into live, per-user state in the browser. You write four handlers; it handles the Kafka consumer, the sockets, reconnects, backpressure and recovery. These pages show what happens in between, and why.
